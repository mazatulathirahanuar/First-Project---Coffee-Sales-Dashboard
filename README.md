## PROJECT OBJECTIVE
The owner of this coffee shop want to view the revenue sales trend of their coffee brand between year 2019 to 2022. So that he can understand the customer preferences of the coffee bean's type and grow more sales in 2023 and 2024.

## DATASET USED
[Coffee Sales Dataset.xlsx](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/32255185/Coffee.Sales.Dataset.xlsx)

## QUESTIONS
1. How many total sales and total of quantity sold for each coffee type (Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, Robusta)?
2. Percentage of customer that subscribed and not subscribe to membership program between 2019 to 2022?
3. How many number of customer who subscribed to membership program for each year?
4. Analyze revenue trend of coffee's sales from January 2019 to August 2022?
5. Create pivot table with an interactive Dashboard of the coffee sales based on the dataset provided.

## PROCESS
1. Verify data for any missing values and sort out the same.		
2. Ensure data is consistent and clean with respect to the data type, data format and values used.									
3. Created pivot tables from the clean dataset according to the question asked.				
4. Merge all pivot tables into one interactive dashboard and apply slicer to make dynamic.

## PROJECT INSIGHT
1. Arabica is the strongest product in terms of quantity sold, while other coffee types have better revenue per unit.
2. Excelsa performs well financially despite selling fewer units than Arabica because Excelsa's average selling value per unit is higher than other beans type.
3. Customer membership is relatively balanced overall since it has almost half of its customers participating in the membership program, but there is still an opportunity of the customer to convert non-members.
4. Membership subscription has the biggest gap in 2021, when non-members significantly outnumbered members.
5. Coffee sales are affected by monthly fluctuations because some months show sharp peaks, while others experience significant drops indicating potential seasonal patterns.

## BUSINESS RECOMMENDATION/SUGGESTION
1. Introduce stronger membership incentives such as discounts, loyalty points, birthday rewards, or member-exclusive promotions.
2. Use promotions and campaigns during months with historically lower sales to improve weak-month performance such as August.
3. Promote high value coffee type by focusing on products such as Exc and Lib that generate strong revenue.
